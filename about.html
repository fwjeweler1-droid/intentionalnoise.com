About Us

Meet the fellow responsible for all this noise

Charles Martin is the independent musician, songwriter, producer and chief troublemaker behind Intentional Noise. A veteran and lifelong tinkerer, he has somehow managed to combine faith, guitars, storytelling, a sense of humor and a remarkable willingness to learn new tricks into a recording project with a higher purpose.

He doesn't claim to have all the answers. He does believe music can ask better questions, make people laugh, and remind us of something worth remembering: love your neighbor. His songs range from reflective and scripture-inspired to satirical and occasionally downright mischievous. If a song makes you think, feel, or grin at an inconvenient moment, the noise was probably intentional.

All of this is accomplished with an independent artist's stubborn determination, a working studio, and enough coffee to keep the ideas moving. Welcome to the office. Mind the dog.
